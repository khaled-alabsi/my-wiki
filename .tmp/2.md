# Business Domain Analysis: Financial Product Recommendation & Compliance Orchestration

## 1. Executive Summary
The provided transcript details a development discussion centered on an **automated financial product recommendation and regulatory compliance assessment workflow**. The core business capability involves orchestrating multiple downstream services to evaluate customer suitability, target market alignment, sustainability criteria, and personal data validation before generating mandatory advisory documents. Key challenges include resolving age-based calculation logic (saving vs. payout phases), handling leap years, detecting first-time product knowledge capture without legacy triggers, and managing policyholder identity resolution for minors. The workflow is tightly coupled with regulatory documentation obligations and requires precise state transitions based on compliance check outcomes.

## 2. Business Context
- **Domain:** Retail Pension & Investment Advisory (German market context implied by terms like *Frühstartdepot*, *AVD*, *BaFin/MiFID II alignment*).
- **Business Problem:** Automate the suitability, target market, and documentation workflow for new or existing clients while ensuring regulatory compliance, accurate age/phase calculations, and proper handling of minor policyholders.
- **Operational Setting:** Frontend advisory interface → Orchestration Service → Backend calculation & compliance engines → Document generation → Archive/Client notification.
- **Delivery Context:** MVP scope with phased rollout; some complex scenarios (e.g., minor transitions) deferred to later releases.

## 3. Key Concepts
| Concept | Business Meaning |
|---------|------------------|
| `Pension Scheme Calculation Date` | Reference date for phase determination & premium calculation, derived from customer age/birthdate. |
| Saving Phase vs. Payout Phase | Lifecycle stage: `<65 years = Saving`, `≥65 years = Payout`. Dictates calculation logic and target market parameters. |
| Model Contract | Predefined investment portfolio template mapped to offensive/defensive asset quotas. |
| Target Market Data | Regulatory product classification & suitability boundaries used by compliance engines. |
| Tamara Service | Internal target market/suitability assessment engine. |
| First-Time vs. Update Knowledge Capture | Regulatory trigger determining whether mandatory documentation/signature is required. |
| Policyholder (Inhaber) vs. Representative | Legal entity owning the account; for minors, the child is the policyholder regardless of who acts on their behalf. |

## 4. Business Process
1. **Trigger:** Client initiates recommendation/advice session via frontend.
2. **Phase Determination:** System calculates `Pension Scheme Calculation Date` based on policyholder birthdate and current age.
3. **Recommendation Generation:** Recommendation service returns offensive/defensive module quotas.
4. **Contract Selection:** Quotas are matched against cached Model Contracts to identify the correct template.
5. **Target Market Extraction:** Selected contract provides target market parameters.
6. **Compliance Checks (Sequential Dependency):** 
   - Tamara (Suitability/Target Market)
   - Sustainability Check
   - Person/Party Validation
   *(Checks may run in parallel technically, but business logic requires prior step completion)*
7. **Result Consolidation:** System evaluates check outcomes against internal thresholds.
8. **Decision Point:** 
   - `Green` (or scores 10/20) → Process continues.
   - `Yellow` → Allowed with operational handling.
   - `Red/Fail` → Process terminates immediately.
9. **Data Model Population & Document Generation:** Fully populated model feeds document renderer; signature requirements applied based on knowledge capture status.
10. **Metadata Update:** Once Depotnummer is known, metadata levels are updated (K → V) via shared endpoint.

## 7. Actors
- **Policyholder:** Natural person owning the account (can be a minor ≥6 years old).
- **Legal Representative:** Acts on behalf of minor policyholders; does not inherit birthdate/legal status for calculations.
- **Advisory System / Frontend User:** Initiates workflow, views results, collects signatures.
- **Orchestration Service (Business Logic Layer):** Coordinates service calls, enforces sequencing, consolidates results.
- **Compliance Engines:** Tamara, Sustainability Checker, Person Data Validator.
- **External/Shared Systems:** MSR (Market Knowledge Repository), TNV (Rights Management), AVD/Sarah (Backend pension processors).
