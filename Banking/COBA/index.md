## Chain traces

Place here: nothing by hand. These are generated by the `coba-chain-tracer` skill — one file per
trigger, each carrying a user story, the call chain, diagrams and reverse-engineered acceptance
criteria. 56 files across four modules:

- `op/chain-traces/` — 31 traces for `ucc-offer-generator`, plus [[module-summary]] and [[scan]].
- `extante/chain-traces/` — 10 traces.
- `chain-traces/` — 10 traces at the COBA root.
- `cancellation/chain-traces/` — 5 traces.

To find one, search the trigger name rather than browsing: `.rag/bin/rag search "<endpoint or
Themenblock>"`.
